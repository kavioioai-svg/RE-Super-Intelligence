# Code of conduct

RESI is maintained by Kavio Pte Ltd and its contributors. We want this to be a welcoming, professional community for everyone, regardless of background or experience.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive technical criticism.
- Credit other people's work, including the tools RESI builds on.
- Keep discussions focused on authorized, responsible research.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory language or personal attacks.
- Publishing other people's private information.
- Requests for help attacking systems, data or people without authorization.
- Sharing proprietary binaries, leaked material or personal data in issues or pull requests.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation for behaviour that breaks this code. Report concerns privately through the repository's **Security → Report a vulnerability** form, marking the report as a conduct issue; reports are handled confidentially.
